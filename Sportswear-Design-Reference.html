STRIDE — Design Reference

Version 1.4 · 17 November 2026. All eight routes in the brief, desktop and mobile, plus every empty, loading, error and success state — twenty-nine frames in total. Version 1.4 moves the toast to bottom centre at four seconds, sets the stepper to 40px, and draws Quick add and the size sheet it opens. Tokens, copy and components are exactly as specified. Mist rectangles stand in for the generated photography. Frames are 1440 desktop and 390 mobile, content held to 1280.
